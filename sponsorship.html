Corporate Sponsorship Opportunities

Organizations and individuals interested in providing additional support can participate through our structured corporate sponsorship levels. Corporate sponsorships directly advance our fundraising efforts for the National Multiple Sclerosis Society and demonstrate a shared commitment to improving the lives of people affected by multiple sclerosis.

Sponsorship Levels

Legacy Sponsor — $10,000 and above
Leadership Sponsor — $5,000 to $9,999
Impact Sponsor — $2,500 to $4,999
Partner Sponsor — $1,000 to $2,499
Friend of the Cause — Up to $999

How to Submit Payment

Donate Online:
Visit our official team page: Youth Driving Change Team Page

Donate by Check:
Checks should be made payable to the National MS Society with 'Youth Driving Change' on the memo line and mailed to:

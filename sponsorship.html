Corporate Sponsorship Opportunities

Organizations and individuals interested in providing additional support can participate through our sponsorship packages. Corporate sponsorships advance our team's fundraising efforts in support of the National Multiple Sclerosis Society and showcase a commitment to funding critical research and community support.

Sponsorship Levels & Benefits

Friend of the Cause

Up to $999
  • Recognition on the event website
  • Recognition in the event program
  • Team photo following the event
  • Recognition in post-event thank-you communications

Partner Sponsor

$1,000 – $2,499

Includes all Friend of the Team benefits, plus:

  • Recognition on sponsor banner
  • Logo on sponsor slideshow
  • Recognition during opening remarks
  • Recognition in event newsletter
  • Logo displayed on the event website

Impact Sponsor

$2,500 – $4,999

Includes all Partner Sponsor benefits, plus:

  • Logo displayed on the event T-shirt
  • Recognition during the educational seminar and dinner
  • Opportunity to include a promotional item in attendee welcome bags
  • Enhanced logo placement on sponsor signage
  • Recognition on event presentation slides

Leadership Sponsor

$5,000 – $9,999

Includes all Impact Sponsor benefits, plus:

  • Prominent logo placement on event signage
  • Priority recognition during major event activities
  • Enlarged logo on the event T-shirt
  • Premium logo placement on the event website
  • Reserved seating at the dinner
  • Recognition during both the opening and closing ceremonies

Legacy Sponsor

$10,000 and above

Includes all Leadership Sponsor benefits, plus:

  • Exclusive recognition as a Legacy Sponsor
  • Premier logo placement on all event materials
  • Largest logo placement on sponsor banner and event signage
  • Opportunity to briefly welcome attendees during the dinner program
  • Opportunity to sponsor a featured event activity
  • Featured sponsor profile on the event website
  • Team photo with event leadership
